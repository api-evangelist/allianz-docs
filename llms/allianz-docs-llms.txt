# Allianz

> Allianz is one of the world's largest insurance and financial services companies, serving over 86 million customers worldwide. Allianz exposes partner-facing developer APIs across multiple product lines — insurance quoting, policy management, claims, certificates, and trade credit — enabling partners and distributors to embed Allianz insurance products into their own platforms. Access generally requires a contractual partnership agreement.

This llms.txt was generated by API Evangelist from the apis.yml catalog and the artifacts in this repository. No official /llms.txt was published on the Allianz developer surfaces at the time of generation.

## APIs

- [Allianz API Connect](https://www.allianz.com.au/about-us/work-with-us/partners/api-connect.html): Australian insurance partner APIs for price estimation, quoting, lead referral, policy completion, and certificate-of-currency retrieval (home, landlord, car). Base URL https://api.allianz.com.au, OAuth2 client-credentials.
- [Allianz Partners API](https://developer.allianz-assistance.ca/): Insurance and assistance product APIs covering policy purchase, change, and cancellation in XML and JSON. Base URL https://api.allianz-assistance.ca. Requires partnership agreement.
- [Allianz Global API](https://global.apis.allianz.com/api-catalog): Enterprise insurance API portal for registered business partners, built on Apigee Edge with OAuth2. Base URL https://apis.allianz.com. Most documentation gated to partners.
